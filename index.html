Giljoo Nam

I am a researcher working toward Physical AI: artificial intelligence that learns the structure and behavior of the 3D physical world. To that end, my research addresses core problems in computer vision and graphics, spanning generative AI, 3D reconstruction and understanding, motion tracking and animation, physically-based forward and inverse rendering, computational imaging, and human modeling.

2020 - now Research Scientist, Meta, United States
2019 Ph.D. in Computer Science, KAIST, Korea (advisor: Min H. Kim)
